Outreach 2026 Gannon Hacks Cyber Club

Inspiring the Next Generation of Cybersecurity Professionals

Gannon Hacks Cyber Club welcomed a group of high school students to our department for an immersive introduction to the world of cybersecurity.

As these young minds prepare to make one of the most important decisions of their lives by choosing a career path, we wanted to give them a front-row seat to see just how dynamic and impactful this field can be.

Threats, Live and In Real Time

We walked them through the fundamentals of Information Assurance & Cybersecurity, explored real-world vulnerabilities, and showcased live simulations of cyberattacks happening across the globe in real time. Watching their eyes light up as they saw threats unfolding on a live threat map was a reminder of why this work matters.

Blue Team, Red Team, and Everything in Between

We also highlighted the vast and growing opportunities in cybersecurity, from the strategic mindset of a Blue Team defender to the analytical precision of a Red Team attacker, and the exciting careers that await those who choose this path.

"Meeting students at this stage, before they've decided on a major, is exactly when we can show them that cybersecurity isn't just a career, it's a way of thinking that applies everywhere." — Professor Ronny Bazan-Antequera, Patron and Technical Advisor, Gannon Hacks Cyber Club

What Awaits at Gannon

Abraham Klogo, the club's president, said it was a pleasure sharing what awaits students at Gannon University, from rigorous technical skills training and continuous hands-on experience, to becoming part of the Gannon Hacks Cyber Club, a community where passion for cybersecurity meets real-world practice.

To every student who visited: the future of cybersecurity needs curious, driven minds like yours. We hope to see you soon!
